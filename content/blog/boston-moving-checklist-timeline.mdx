Short answer: start your Boston moving checklist eight weeks out. Here's the exact week-by-week plan, so nothing gets discovered too late.

## The Full Boston Moving Checklist, Week by Week

Work backward from your move date. Some of these steps compress if you're short on time, but the order stays the same regardless of how many weeks you actually have.

| Weeks Out | Key Tasks |
|---|---|
| 8 weeks | Set budget, set move date, start decluttering, get quotes |
| 6 weeks | Book movers, order packing supplies, research schools/childcare if relevant |
| 4 weeks | Start packing non-essentials, use up frozen food, schedule furniture disassembly |
| 2 weeks | Confirm movers and parking permit, submit change of address, transfer utilities |
| 1 week | Pack essentials box, finish packing, confirm building/elevator logistics |
| Moving day | Final walkthrough, crew arrival, load, drive, unload, tip |

The eight-week mark isn't arbitrary. [moveBuddha's moving checklist](https://www.movebuddha.com/blog/moving-checklist/) recommends starting there specifically because it's the point where decluttering, quote-gathering, and booking can happen in the right sequence instead of all at once under deadline pressure.

## 8 Weeks Out: Set the Budget and Start Cutting Down

Decide what you're spending before you decide who you're hiring. A number in mind keeps every later quote in context, instead of anchoring to whatever the first company tells you.

Then start decluttering, and do it before you request quotes, not after. A quote based on your current apartment, including the furniture you're about to sell or donate, isn't the quote you'll actually pay. Sort into keep, sell, donate, and toss piles room by room. Our [guide to decluttering before a Boston move](/blog/declutter-before-a-boston-move) walks through the full sorting method and where in the city to actually drop things off.

This is also when to start comparing movers, not booking them yet. Check for a US DOT number, read recent reviews, and rule out anyone who won't give a written estimate. The [American Moving and Storage Association](https://www.moving.org) publishes a rundown of the same red flags federal regulators use to spot moving fraud, which is worth a skim before you sign anything. Our [guide to choosing a moving company in Boston](/blog/how-to-choose-a-moving-company-boston) covers the specific version of those red flags that shows up most in this market.

## 6 Weeks Out: Book Your Movers Before the Good Dates Go

Lock in your movers now, once your declutter pass gives you an accurate sense of what's actually moving. Booking earlier than that tends to mean paying for a crew size or truck sized for stuff that's no longer coming with you.

We see this pattern constantly on Boston jobs: people book movers off a rough guess, then call back a week later to downsize the order once the donation pile is actually gone. It's a normal call to make, but it's one fewer call if the declutter pass happens first. Once you do book, get the number in writing. NoTimeMover locks your price in at booking, fully insured, with no surprise stair fees added at the door, so this is the point where your budget stops being an estimate and starts being a number you can plan around.

Boston-specific timing matters here too. [Book at least 3-4 weeks out](/blog/how-far-in-advance-to-book-movers-boston) for a normal weekday move, longer for weekends, and well past that minimum if you're anywhere near September 1st.

If you're bringing kids or pets, this is also the week to start on school enrollment, vet record transfers, or childcare logistics for moving day itself. Both take longer to sort out than people expect once you're this close to the date.

## 4 Weeks Out: Pack What You Don't Touch Daily

Start with the stuff you could live without for a month: off-season clothes, books, decor, the guest room. Leave the kitchen and bathroom essentials for later, since you still need to use them.

Order or collect boxes now instead of the week before. [Free moving boxes](/blog/free-moving-boxes-boston) are easiest to find at this point, before every other mover in your building or on your block is hunting for the same liquor-store boxes during peak season. Buy dish boxes and packing paper for fragile items specifically. Regular boxes crush glassware; dish boxes have double-layer walls built for the weight.

This is also the week to schedule furniture disassembly if you've got a bed frame, sectional, or bulky dresser that won't survive a Boston stairwell fully assembled. Our [furniture disassembly guide](/blog/furniture-disassembly-boston-move) covers what to take apart ahead of time versus what to leave for the crew.

## Do You Need a Parking Permit for Moving Day?

In most of the city, yes. Boston requires a temporary no-parking permit for moving trucks in dense neighborhoods, and it needs to be filed with the city roughly [10-14 business days ahead](/blog/boston-moving-truck-parking-permit) of your move date, not the week of.

Skip this step and your crew is stuck circling the block or parking illegally, which slows down the job and risks a ticket that's genuinely avoidable with two weeks of lead time. Triple-deckers and walk-ups on narrow streets in Somerville, Allston, and the North End feel this the most, since there's rarely a driveway to fall back on. Set a reminder for this specific task now, four to six weeks out, so it doesn't slip through while you're focused on packing.

## 2 Weeks Out: Utilities, Address Changes, and Final Confirmations

Transfer or schedule your utilities to start at the new place and stop at the old one, ideally landing the shutoff a day or two after you've actually left. Our [utilities checklist for a Boston move](/blog/utilities-checklist-boston-move) breaks down electric, gas, water, and internet by what's typically the tenant's responsibility versus the landlord's under Massachusetts law.

Submit your change of address with USPS and update it with your bank, employer, and any subscriptions. Massachusetts also requires an RMV address update within 30 days of moving if you're staying in-state, which is easy to forget once the move itself is behind you. Our [change of address checklist](/blog/change-of-address-checklist-boston) covers the full list.

Call your movers to reconfirm the date, time, and any building requirements. If you're in a high-rise or a building with 6+ units, this is also the deadline to make sure a certificate of insurance is on file with the building if one's required, since some buildings won't grant freight elevator access without it.

## What Goes in Your Essentials Box?

Pack it last, during the final week, and keep it with you instead of putting it on the truck. It should hold anything you'd need in the first 24 hours in a new place before a single other box gets opened: toiletries, phone chargers, a change of clothes, basic tools, medications, and pet food or supplies if you've got a pet along for the move.

Finish the rest of your packing this week too, working room by room so nothing gets left for a rushed morning-of scramble. Label every box by room, not just contents, since that's what actually speeds up the unload. For the day itself, our [hour-by-hour Boston moving day timeline](/blog/boston-moving-day-timeline) walks through what a typical [local move](/local-moving) looks like from crew arrival to the final walkthrough, so you know roughly when to expect each stage.

## What Changes If You're Moving Around September 1st?

Add two to three weeks to every deadline on this list. September 1st is Boston's biggest single moving day, the date when a huge share of the city's leases turn over at once, and it compresses an entire year's worth of demand into one week. Movers, trucks, and parking permits all book out further ahead than any other stretch of the year, and last-minute availability essentially disappears.

If your move lands anywhere near that date, treat 8 weeks out as your absolute latest start, not your ideal one. Our [full guide to moving on September 1st](/blog/moving-september-1-boston) covers what else changes that week, from pricing to street congestion, beyond just the booking deadline.
